Statutory Legal Protections

Rights of Persons with Disabilities (RPwD) Act 2016

A comprehensive guide to your fundamental legal rights, mandatory accessibility standards, and statutory quasi-judicial grievance mechanisms across India.

⚖️ Core Statutory Protections Under the Law

Section 3: Equality & Non-Discrimination

The appropriate Government shall ensure that the person with disability enjoys the right to equality, life with dignity and respect for his or her integrity equally with others. No person with disability shall be discriminated on the ground of disability, unless it is shown that the impugned act or omission is a proportionate means of achieving a legitimate aim.

Section 20: Non-Discrimination in Employment & Promotion

No Government establishment shall discriminate against any person with disability in any matter relating to employment. No promotion shall be denied to a person merely on the ground of disability. If an employee acquires a disability during service, they cannot be dispensed with or reduced in rank.

Section 34: 4% Mandatory Reservation in Government Jobs

Mandatory minimum 4% reservation in all government establishments, public sector undertakings, and autonomous bodies across: (a) Blindness and low vision (1%), (b) Deaf and hard of hearing (1%), (c) Locomotor disability (1%), and (d) Autism, intellectual disability, specific learning disability, and multiple disabilities (1%).

Section 40 & 46: Mandatory Accessibility of Public Premises & Websites

The Central Government shall formulate rules laying down standards of accessibility for the physical environment, transportation, information and communication including appropriate technologies and systems (WCAG / GIGW compliance). No building plan shall be approved without compliance to accessibility norms.

📋 21 Recognized Disabilities Under RPwD Act 2016

The RPwD Act 2016 expanded recognized disabilities from 7 to 21 categories, enabling nationwide welfare benefits via the UDID card:

1. Blindness
2. Low-Vision
3. Leprosy Cured Persons
4. Hearing Impairment (Deaf & Hard of Hearing)
5. Locomotor Disability
6. Dwarfism
7. Intellectual Disability
8. Mental Illness
9. Autism Spectrum Disorder
10. Cerebral Palsy
11. Muscular Dystrophy
12. Chronic Neurological Conditions
13. Specific Learning Disabilities
14. Multiple Sclerosis
15. Speech and Language Disability
16. Thalassemia
17. Hemophilia
18. Sickle Cell Disease
19. Multiple Disabilities including Deaf-blindness
20. Acid Attack Victims
21. Parkinson's Disease

📝 How to File a Discrimination Complaint with CCPD

Step 1: Write a factual representation addressed to the Head of the organization/establishment detailing the violation. Step 2: If no corrective action is taken within 30 days, file an online complaint at the CCPD Portal (ccdisabilities.nic.in/online-complaint). Step 3: Attach copy of your UDID Card or Disability Certificate and proof of representation sent. Step 4: The Court of the Chief Commissioner will issue a notice to the respondent establishment. Step 5: Attend online video hearing or receive formal order directing remedial action.
